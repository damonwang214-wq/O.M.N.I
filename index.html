O.M.N.I.

Obviously More than a Normal Intelligence

A voice-controlled AI butler with opinions, a dry wit and a luminous core. Built as a school project — with designs on becoming an app.

Talk to O.M.N.I. About the project

Capabilities

Everything runs in the browser — no installs, no servers, no API keys. Just open the page and talk.

🎙️

Voice input

Speak to O.M.N.I. and the Web Speech API turns your words into text — hands-free, like the real thing.

🔊

Spoken replies

O.M.N.I. answers out loud with a deep, measured voice — pitch and pace tuned to sound like a butler, not a sat-nav.

🧠

A personality

A rule-based brain handles greetings, jokes, maths, the time, live weather, opening websites — and remembers your name.

💻

Runs anywhere

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Double-click and it works — or host it free on GitHub Pages for the whole school to see.

How to use it

Three steps. No manual required.

Open the Talk page

Hit "Talk to O.M.N.I." up top. Allow microphone access when the browser asks.

Speak or type

Tap the mic and talk, or just type. Try "hello", "what time is it", or "tell me a joke".

Listen

O.M.N.I. replies in text — and out loud, if the voice is on. Say "help" anytime for the full list.

Heads up: voice input & speech need Chrome or Edge with a microphone. Everywhere else, the typing bar still works perfectly.
